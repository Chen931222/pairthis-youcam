Privacy and how it works

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (YouCam hackathon version)

The clothes you add are saved in your own browser. No account, no cookies, no ads. This contest version keeps no usage stats. Some features connect to other services, all listed below; one of them, “Try it on you”, sends your photo out.

Data and privacy

When it connects to other services

  • Weather: today's pick asks open-meteo.com for the weather in the city you chose. What it sends is the city's latitude and longitude. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Background removal: the first time you add a piece or use “Compare in store”, your browser downloads the background-removal model (about 80 MB) from staticimgly.com. Your photo isn't sent anywhere; the background is removed on your device.
  • Pasting a GU or UNIQLO link: this site's server looks up the name and price for you; the product images are downloaded by your browser straight from gu-global.com and uniqlo.com, so they can see your IP address.
  • Pasting a link from another brand: this site's server reads both the product page and the images, so the brand sees this site's server.
  • Try it on you: the photo you add is stored only in this browser. When you tap “Try this on”, that photo and the piece's image pass through this site's server to the YouCam (Perfect Corp.) API, which makes the try-on photo, and the result is saved back in this browser. This site's server doesn't keep the photos. YouCam's API documentation (v1.8) says uploaded files and results stay on their servers for 24 hours and are then deleted automatically; we couldn't find the same sentence in the current documentation, so YouCam's API terms are what count. Try-on photos are made by AI and may differ from the real thing on you.

All of these services can see your IP address, as any connection does.

Usage stats and feedback

This contest version keeps no usage stats and has no feedback button.

How to delete

  • One piece: open it and tap “Delete”. It goes to the closet's “Trash” first (shown only when something is in it). After 30 days it's deleted for good the next time you open the site; “Delete for good” in the trash deletes it right away.
  • Your try-on photo: on an item page, under “Try it on you”, tap “Delete photo”; the try-ons made with that photo are deleted too. Delete a single try-on with “Delete this try-on”.
  • Everything: clear this site's browser data.

Storage and backups

  • Your browser: the clothes' images (after background removal), names, prices, notes, wear log, saved outfits and adjustments on the form are all kept in this browser's storage and aren't sent anywhere automatically. This site doesn't keep a cloud backup for you.
  • iPhone: Safari may clear this data if you don't open the site for about a week; opening it from the Home Screen doesn't. It's safer to use Share → “Add to Home Screen” before you start adding.
  • Backup file: “Export backup” under “Backup” at the bottom of the Outfits page downloads a ZIP that stays with you and never goes through the cloud. Export before switching phones or clearing browser data, then tap “Import backup” on the new phone and choose the file.
  • Encrypted backup: tap “Export encrypted” and set a password. The site doesn't keep this password; if you forget it, the backup can't be opened.

How it works

  • Taking photos: hang the piece up, or lay it flat and smooth out wrinkles before the photo; the cutout comes out cleaner. With a product link, pick the shot of the piece on its own; on a model shot, the person stays in too.
  • Category: you can skip it when adding; it guesses from the name and shape, and the closet tile says “Category guessed”. If it's wrong, open the piece and change it.
  • Outfit preview: the form uses flat-lay photos, so the drape will look different when worn. Sizes are inferred from the photo's outline; if a piece sits wrong, drag it.
  • When you don't have enough clothes for an outfit: today's pick and “Another outfit” borrow the missing categories from the demo closet, and the reasons say how many were borrowed; add a few of your own and the whole outfit is yours.
  • Try it on you: only tops and outerwear can be tried on. Use a photo from the chest up, facing the camera, with your shoulders and whole face in view and only you in it. Each try-on uses YouCam API units; this site allows up to 100 a day from the same internet connection.

Terms of use

  • Free for now, provided as is. The site may pause or break, and data kept in the browser may be lost; export a backup regularly if it matters to you.
  • Brands' product images belong to their owners and are only for organizing your own closet.
  • Don't upload photos of other people or photos with other people's faces.
  • Don't call this site's server in bulk from scripts; too many requests get temporarily blocked.
